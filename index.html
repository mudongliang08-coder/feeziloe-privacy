Feeziloe Ads Reporting Privacy Notice

Effective date: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Feeziloe Ads Reporting is an internal tool used to read, analyze, and report on Amazon Advertising data for the Feeziloe store. It supports internal advertising operations and is not a public advertising management service. This notice describes the information processed through the tool.

2. Information We Process

The tool processes Amazon Advertising profile IDs; campaign IDs and campaign details; ad group details; keywords; product targets; search terms; impressions; clicks; advertising spend; attributed sales; advertising placements; and ASINs and SKUs. It also combines these records with product cost and profit information supplied by the operator.

3. How We Use Information

We use this information for advertising performance and search term analysis, keyword optimization, negative keyword suggestions, bid optimization suggestions, advertising budget analysis, product profitability analysis, and internal business reports.

4. Data Storage and Security

Downloaded advertising reports, analysis files, and API credentials are stored on the operator's local Windows computer. These local files are not synchronized or backed up to a cloud storage service. API credentials are kept separate from the public website. Client secrets, refresh tokens, and access tokens are not published or committed to GitHub. Advertising data submitted to Codex or Workbuddy for analysis may be processed by those cloud AI services under their applicable terms and settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Amazon Ads API is used to authorize access to and read advertising data. Codex and Workbuddy may process advertising data submitted for internal analysis and reporting. GitHub Pages hosts this public notice; the notice itself does not include advertising data or API credentials.

6. Data Sharing

Advertising data is not sold or used for unrelated commercial purposes. When advertising data is submitted to Codex or Workbuddy, those service providers may process the submitted information for the requested internal analysis. Access to advertising data is otherwise limited to the operator and services needed for this tool.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Downloaded advertising reports and related analysis files are normally kept for approximately three months, depending on operational needs. The operator deletes local copies when they are no longer needed. The operator can request deletion or revocation of the application's Amazon Ads API authorization by contacting the email address below. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not itself delete previously downloaded records; those records are handled under the deletion process described above.

8. Changes to This Notice

We may update this notice when the tool's functions, data processing, service providers, or retention practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an updated notice is published.
